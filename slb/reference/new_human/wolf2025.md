# wolf2025: Wolf, Leippe et al. 2025 Mol Syst Biol, SLC genetic interaction map (HCT 116)

doi 10.1038/s44320-025-00105-5 (CC BY), PMID 40355755, GEO GSE269905. Parser: `src/slbench/sources/staging/wolf2025.py`.

## Design and what is used
- There are two SLC x SLC screens of the same 33,153 gene pairs (268 genes after HGNC renames, 1.41% paralog pairs = 467):
  - enCas12a: 4 sublibraries, 3 replicates, weeks 1/2/3/5
  - SpCas9: 1 library, 2 replicates at weeks 1-3 and 1 at week 4
  - Both are HCT 116 in standard high-glucose medium; every SLC x SLC GEO sample has treatment "--".
- The 4 growth conditions (glu/gal/antimycin/hypoxia) exist only in the SLC x enzyme arm. That arm is not used, and neither is the Cas12a metal pilot (EV1).
- Measurements come from the authors' scoring sheets (Dataset EV2 = Cas12a, EV3 = Cas9; Springer static-content xlsx). Score = week-3 dLFC (the authors' scoring time point; negative = SL), signif = padj. Each nuclease is a separate row (CRISPR-Cas12a / CRISPR-KO), context "HCT 116".
- Control pairs (HDAC1/2, MAPK1/3, MAP2K1/2, STAG1/2, BCL2L1/MCL1, plus SLC25A28/37) are kept as ordinary measurements. The Cas12a sheet lists them once per sublibrary; `finalize` merges the copies. BCL2L1-MCL1 has conflicting calls across copies, so it becomes null.
- HGNC: all 268 symbols resolve. 15 are renamed (e.g. MFSD14A->SLC71A1, SLC66A5->MPDU1, TMEM104->SLC38A12).

## Label rule
- The authors' week-3 call is used. The positive call is `synthetic_lethal`:
  - Cas12a requires padj < 0.1 in each of the 3 replicates and dLFC < -0.3.
  - Cas9 requires pooled padj < 0.005 and dLFC < -0.3.
  - Both then apply a saturation filter and a week-1->3 trend filter.
- Negative: `neutral`, |dLFC| below the screen median, and padj > 0.1.
- Everything else is null. This includes synthetic viable pairs and the 164 Cas12a pairs that pass padj/dLFC but were removed by the authors' filters.
- Only Cas12a is labelled (`LABELLED_ARMS`): 26 pos (4 of them non-SLC controls), 16,588 neg, 0.08% hit rate. 42% of positives are paralog pairs.
- Cas9 rows keep label = null. Calling `load(tuple(EV))` labels both arms: Cas9 has 97 pos. A build-style collapse of both gives 71 pos / 24,707 neg; 10 pairs are positive in both arms and 42 pairs conflict (pos vs neg).

## Rejected alternative: re-score from counts, z <= -3
Additive GI (spidr recipe) re-scored from counts gives 252 Cas12a and 166 Cas9 hits. It was rejected for three reasons:
- The hits are dominated by 6 essential SLCs (SLC7A1, SLC35B1, SLC30A9, SLC25A3, MTCH2, LETM1).
- Single-gene fitness alone predicts them at AUROC 0.90 / 0.92.
- Cas9 recovers the Cas12a hits at only 0.52.

Genome-wide, the two nucleases barely correlate:
- Spearman 0.01 for re-scored GI and 0.03 for the authors' dLFC.
- Restricting to pairs of non-essential genes still gives about 0.5.

The authors' filtered calls are the part that replicates.

## Checks
- **Replicate GI (`replicates()`)**:
  - Each replicate is scored against its own plasmid-input replicate.
  - With a shared mean input, replicates share the input sampling noise, and this inflates agreement. The effect is large for Cas9, whose input depth is low: Spearman 0.28 with a shared input vs 0.05 with independent inputs.
  - The Cas9-Control spike-in sublibrary is dropped from the counts because its input depth is 10x the rest.
- **Own replication (independent inputs, split rule z<=-3 on one replicate scored by the mean of the rest)**:
  - Cas12a: 0.81 / 0.89 / 0.71.
  - Cas9: 0.63 / 0.65. With a shared input these become 0.72 / 0.74, but that is inflated.
  - Single-replicate AUROC for the final Cas12a labels is 0.96-0.99. This is optimistic, because the labels come from the same data.
- **Cas9 vs Cas12a (headline check)**:
  - Cas12a calls scored by Cas9: 0.77 on author dLFC and 0.67 on re-scored GI (26 pos). Without the control pairs: 0.73 / 0.67 (22 pos).
  - Cas9 calls scored by Cas12a: 0.63 / 0.63 (97 pos). Without the control pairs: 0.61 (93 pos).
- **Cross-study**: no other SLB study was run in HCT 116. About 109 pairs overlap other lines. Scoring our labels with the mean percentile across other lines gives 0.79 (Cas12a, 9 pos) and 0.77 (Cas9, 11 pos). These are mostly the control pairs; without them there are at most 7 positives, which is too few to judge.
- **Fitness (AUROC of -(f_a+f_b) for the labels)**:
  - Cas12a: screen singles 0.61, DepMap HCT 116 0.70, DepMap pan 0.79. Without controls: 0.71 / 0.77.
  - Cas9: 0.64-0.68.
  - None of these is above 0.8.

## Verdict
- **Cas12a: include.** It passes on its own replicates and on the independent nuclease.
- **Cas9: measurements-only.** Both gates are below 0.65, although it is not contradicted (not below 0.6).
- **Caveats**:
  - The Cas12a hit rate is very low (0.08%).
  - Nuclease agreement is confined to the strong hits.
  - The authors attribute some sign flips to clonal differences between the Cas9 and Cas12a HCT 116 clones (e.g. SLC25A1-SLC39A1).
