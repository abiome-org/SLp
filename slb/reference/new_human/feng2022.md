# feng2022 — Feng, Tang, Dede et al. 2022 Sci Adv (Chen/Hart labs), HEK293A

Parser: `src/slbench/sources/staging/feng2022.py`. It uses the engine in `desjardins2026.py`.

**Data.** figshare 19398332, all 5 files; the counts are in Table S1. The authors' Table S2 gives BAGEL
binary essential calls, not an interaction score, so the screens are re-scored from counts.

**Design.**
- Batches XF498, XF646, XF804 and XF821 each have their own WT arm, and every query is scored against the
  WT arm of its own batch.
- TP53BP1 (batch XF443) has no WT arm. It is scored against the mean of the 4 WT arms.
- SETD2 is in the BF matrix but not in the counts, so it is not scored.
- Context "HEK293-A" (CVCL_6910).

**Label rule.** Same engine and rule as desjardins2026: z <= -4 in the pooled score, z < 0 in both
replicates, LFC_KO < -0.5, and no hit for >= 3 queries.

**Why measurements-only.**
- Scoring one WT batch against another (no query at all) gives 83% of the query hit rate at z <= -3,
  51% at -4 and 20% at -5.
- The same genes hit 5–6 unrelated queries across batches: PRDX1, DDX19A, PPP2R3C, C17orf70, BRIP1.
- ARID1A–ARID1B is absent (z = +1.5).
- The technical split-half (0.86/0.85) passes only because the replicates share the clone, T0 and WT arm.

**Results.**
- 229,133 pairs, 187 pos (0.08%, below the plausible range), 155,868 neg.
- Fitness AUROC 0.66. 38 of 17,815 symbols unresolved.

**Cross-study.** There is no same-line overlap. Different-line overlap with desjardins2026 is at chance
(0.51/0.46).

**Verdict.** Measurements-only.
