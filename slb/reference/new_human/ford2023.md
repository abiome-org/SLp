# ford2023 — Ford et al. 2023 Sci Rep (10.1038/s41598-023-33329-2, CC BY 4.0)

**Design.** Dual-sgRNA Cas9, all-by-all over 26 genes (CDK1-20, CDK11A/B, AR, EZH2, PARP1, PRMT5, TGFBR1) plus
non-targeting (`ntc`) and AAVS1-cutting (`ntc-AAV`) controls. 4 sgRNAs per gene, both orientations (32 guide pairs
per unordered gene pair). Lines: MDA-MB-231, MDA-MB-468, Hs 578T; 2 replicates x 4 timepoints (T3 ... T28), with no plasmid counts.
The paper's "378 pairs" includes the 2 control "genes" (28 choose 2). There are 325 real gene x gene pairs per line (975 rows), 210 (65%) of them paralogs.

**Source file.** `data/raw/ford2023/41598_2023_33329_MOESM1_ESM.xlsx`. Parsed sheets are cached as parquet in `data/raw/ford2023/_parsed/`.
- `Interaction_Scores`: the authors' `new_pi` per line (406 rows per line, including self and control rows).
- `*_Dual_CRISPR_Counts`: raw counts.
- The "Genetic interaction score" in `Physical_interaction_evidence` is the within-line z of new_pi (checked: AR-CDK1 in MDA-MB-231 is -3.18). Multi-line
  entries are averages. The authors highlight pairs at about |z| >= 2, which is not stringent.

**Measurement.** score = new_pi (negative = SL). There is no p-value (signif null).

**Label rule.** Within-line z of new_pi over the 325 gene pairs. Positive: z <= -3 AND own additive GI < 0 in both
replicates (the same double-check spidr2025 uses). Negative: |z| < 1. Everything else is null. Result: 7 positives (0.72%), 747 negatives.
6 of the 13 pairs with z <= -3 fail the both-replicates-negative check.

**replicates().** Follows the spidr_replicate_gi recipe per line. T28 replicate vs mean T3; pairs below the 2% T3 quantile are dropped; LFC is centred on control x control (ntc + AAVS1),
f_g = median gene x control, and GI = median over guide pairs of LFC - f_a - f_b.

**Findings.**
- Replicate agreement is poor. GI Spearman between replicates is 0.17 / 0.18 / 0.09 (231 / 468 / Hs578T). Raw T28 count correlations are
  only 0.54-0.68, and MDA-MB-468 T3 replicates correlate at 0.29. The z<=-3 split-rule AUROC per line is unstable (0.41-0.94 on 1-6 positives). Pooled over lines it is 0.69 / 0.70.
  The authors' pi z<=-3 set (13) scored by each single replicate gives 0.68 / 0.70.
- Fitness confounding: the AUROC of -(f_a+f_b) (DepMap) for the labels is 0.92, so this is flagged. All positives involve essential genes
  (CDK1, CDK7, CDK9, CDK12, PRMT5, CDK11B, CDK4).
- Cross-study: the only same-line overlap is flister2025 MDA-MB-231 (4 pairs). Its positive, CDK4-CDK6, has pi z = -2.17 here, so it is labelled null.
  Evidence from other lines is mixed: ito2021, chou2025 and flister2025 give 0.68-0.90. harle2025's CDK8-CDK19 positives (4 lines) are not
  recovered, because CDK8-CDK19 pi is about 0 or positive here.
- All 26 symbols resolve through HGNC.

**Verdict: measurements-only.** There are few positives, replication is weak, and fitness confounding is strong.
MDA-MB-231 overlaps kim2025 (another agent), which was not checked here.
