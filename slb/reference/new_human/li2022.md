# li2022: Li et al. 2022 Nat Commun 13:2469 (Sellers lab CombiMiniLib)

doi 10.1038/s41467-022-30196-9, CC BY 4.0. Parser: `src/slbench/sources/staging/li2022.py`.
Raw files: `data/raw/li2022/` (Supplementary Data 1-11 = MOESM4-14; figshare 19565902 Raw_Data.zip).
`_parsed/counts.parquet` caches Supplementary Data 9.

## Design
- 616 genes x 6 spCas9 guides (a separate enCas12a guide set). The table has 423 real gene pairs; the paper's
  "454" also counts 15 gene x `<GENE>_copy` self-pair controls and a chr2 intergenic control, and the GEMINI
  table lists 439. AAVS1 is the cutting control. Each pair has 18 guide combinations: 3 A-guides (left) x 3
  B-guides (right), plus the reverse orientation built from the other 3 guides of each gene. The two
  orientations therefore use disjoint guide sets.
- Ten architectures were screened in IPC-298: spCas9 tracrRNA combinations SPCR1/VCR1/WCR2/WCR3/SCR27/SCR43,
  enCas12a, and spCas9-saCas9. The spCas9-saCas9 arm is not a new screen: it is the published Ito et al.
  2021 data. VCR1-WCR3, WCR2-WCR3 and enCas12a were also screened in PK-1 and MEL-JUSO. There are 3
  biological replicates; each library has its own pDNA reference, and no T0 was sequenced.

## Decisions
- I used only the **VCR1-WCR3** architecture. The paper calls it the best library (strongest pan-essential
  depletion, balanced guide positions, most synergistic pairs), and it is the only top library screened in
  all three lines. I did not pool architectures, because the SCR27/SCR43 libraries are inert according to
  the paper and pooling would mix nucleases. The other architectures serve as the replication check
  (`architectures()`).
- Scoring follows the spidr recipe on the counts. Guide pairs whose pDNA count is below the 2% quantile are
  dropped. LFC is centred on AAVS1xAAVS1. The single-gene effect is the median LFC of gene x AAVS1.
  GI = LFC - f_a - f_b, taking the median over the 18 guide pairs. The score is the z-value of the pooled GI
  within each line.
- Label rule: positive if z <= -3 and GI < 0 in all 3 replicates; negative if |z| < 1; everything else is
  null. The authors publish GEMINI sensitive scores only, with no per-pair FDR or binary call. In MOESM14 a
  negative score means synergy, and -GEMINI ranks our labels at AUROC 1.0.
- `replicates()` returns gi_rep1..3 (biological replicates) plus gi_orient1/2 (the guide-disjoint
  orientations) for each line.

## Results (details in li2022.json)
- Positives per line: IPC-298 12/423 (377 negatives), PK-1 9/423 (359 negatives), MEL-JUSO 6/423
  (326 negatives). In total 27 positives and 1062 negatives over 1269 pair-line rows; positive rate 2.1%.
  586 genes; 52% of pairs are paralogs in the SLB paralog table. All symbols resolve to HGNC, 13 of them
  after a rename.
- Own replication:
  - Replicate split (rule labels from 1 replicate, scored by the mean of the other 2): AUROC 0.93-1.00.
  - Orientation split: AUROC 0.74-1.00. The lowest is MEL-JUSO, orient1 scored by orient2.
  - Single replicates score the final labels at >= 0.994.
  - Whole-distribution replicate Spearman is only 0.40-0.52, because most pairs are neutral.
- Architectures: SPCR1-WCR3, WCR2-WCR3, WCR3-VCR1 and enCas12a recover the labels at AUROC 0.89-1.00.
  SCR27/SCR43 do not (0.27-0.49), which is expected for inert tracrRNAs.
- Cross-study, same line:
  - **Ito 2021** (ryanlab zdLFC). Per line, their score on our labels / our score on theirs:
    - IPC-298: 0.967 (5 pos) / 0.981 (5 pos)
    - PK-1: 0.940 (5) / 0.852 (7)
    - MEL-JUSO: 0.955 (4) / n/a (0 Ito positives in the overlap)
    - Pooled over the 3 lines: 0.950 (14) / 0.897 (12)
  - **Chou 2025** MelJuso (Hart lab, enCas12a; independent): 0.886 (5) / 0.790 (15).
  - Other lines and other studies are weaker evidence: medians are 0.79-0.97 for Harle, Flister, Parrish and
    Chou. Thompson and CHyMErA overlaps are tiny.
- Fitness: DepMap -(f_a+f_b) gives AUROC 0.773 overall. PK-1 alone reaches 0.826 and is flagged (> 0.8).

## Caveats
- **Ito 2021 is not independent.** It comes from the same lab (T. Ito is an author on both papers) and used
  the same three lines, probably the same Cas9 derivatives. Guide overlap is partial: 681 of the 3,674 Li
  spCas9 guides (19%) also appear as spCas9 guides in the Ito spCas9-saCas9 library (Minilib_Annotation.rds
  vs SpSa_Annotation.rds). The saCas9 guides differ. Chou 2025 is the only fully independent same-line check,
  and it has just 5 of our positives in the overlap.
- **Positives are mostly designed controls.** 19 of the 27 positives are the authors' designed pan-essential
  paralog positive-control pairs (Supplementary Data 3, picked from DepMap paralog-dependency evidence). The
  remaining positives are UBE2A|UBE2B (3 lines), MAP2K1|MAP2K2, POC1A|POC1B, CDK4|CDK6, BCL2L1|MCL1 and
  DUSP4|DUSP6. Labels could leak into any feature built from DepMap co-dependency.
- The dataset is small: 423 pairs, and 4-7 positives per line fall in each cross-study overlap. No overlap
  reaches 20 positives, so no contradiction test is possible.

Verdict: **include**.
