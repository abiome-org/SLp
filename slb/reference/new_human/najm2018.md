# najm2018 — Najm et al. 2018 Nat Biotechnol 36:179 "Big Papi"

**License.** The files are Springer Nature journal supplementary material with no open licence stated.
SLB ships derived tables only.

**Data.** MOESM27 zip (kept unextracted; the parser reads it directly). It contains per-construct reads for 3
replicates plus pDNA.

**Libraries loaded.** Both are drug-free KO screens:
- **SynLet** (Supp. Tables 2–4): 25 genes, 3 guides per gene and enzyme, 18 constructs per pair across both
  orientations. Lines 786-O, A375, A549, HT-29, MEL-JUSO and OVCAR-8, day 21. A549, HT-29 and MEL-JUSO have
  2 replicates; the other three have 3.
- **Apoptosis** (Supp. Tables 5–7): 32 BCL2-family and caspase genes, 4 guides per gene and enzyme,
  32 constructs per pair. No-drug arm only, in MEL-JUSO (3 replicates) and OVCAR-8 (1 replicate).

Not used: the drug arms of Table 6 and the TsgOnco CRISPRa/HA1E library (Table 9). U6 carries the 20-nt Sp
guides and H1 the 21-nt Sa guides.

**Scoring.** Re-scored from counts with the spidr_replicate_gi recipe, using guide-level singles.
- LFC vs pDNA, dropping the bottom 2% of pDNA.
- Centre on CD81/HPRT-intron cutting-control pairs.
- f = median of guide x cutting control.
- GI = LFC - f_U6 - f_H1, then the median over constructs.
- score = GI computed on the replicate-mean LFC.
- 6T (non-cutting) and EEF2 are dropped.

**Label rule.** Positive: the authors' per-line SynLet q < 0.05 and GI < 0. Negative: SynLet q > 0.25, buffering
q > 0.25 and |GI| below the line/library median. Everything else is null.

786-O is kept as measurements only (labels null). The authors call nothing there, and its replicates and
orientations do not reproduce each other's tails (split-rule AUROC 0.29–0.59).

When a pair is measured in both libraries (15 BCL2-family pairs in MEL-JUSO and OVCAR-8), finalize merges it and
keeps the label only if both libraries agree. This drops MEL-JUSO BCL2L1-BCL2L2 and BCL2-BCL2L1, which are
SynLet-called but null in the Apoptosis library.

**Results.**
- 2,762 rows, 781 unique pairs, 51 genes.
- 17 positives, a rate of 0.62% (0.69% over labelled lines). 1,208 negatives.
- 15/17 positives are paralogs. By line: A375 3, A549 2, HT-29 5, MEL-JUSO 3, OVCAR-8 4.
- The positive pairs are BCL2L1-MCL1 (x5), MAPK1-MAPK3 (x5), BCL2L1-BCL2L2 (x3), AKT1-AKT2, MAP2K1-MAP2K2,
  MCL1-MTOR and BRCA2-PARP1.
- Own replication:
  - A rule defined on one Sp/Sa orientation (robust z <= -3) and scored by the other gives 0.80 (69 pos, pooled).
  - The replicate-to-replicate 2%-tail split averages 0.77 (0.77–0.99 in SynLet lines with signal).
  - Author calls scored by a single orientation give 0.97, and by a single replicate 0.99. Both are optimistic.
- Cross-study, same line, included sources pooled: their score recovers our labels at 0.81 (11 pos), and our
  score recovers their labels at 0.83 (9 pos). By source: Dede 0.75/0.96, Chou 0.88/0.75, Flister 1.0/1.0.
  Ito 2021 disagrees (0.22 with 4 pos), but SLB already excludes Ito because other studies contradict it.
- Fitness-only AUROC: 0.71 (DepMap mean), 0.71 (DepMap line), 0.74 (screen singles). The paralog flag alone gives
  0.92 because the panel was designed around paralog pairs.

**Verdict: include.** Evidence comes from orientation replication and same-line cross-study. The source is
small and repeats the same few pairs across lines, so it adds little family diversity.
