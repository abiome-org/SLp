# najm2023 — Najm, DeWeirdt et al. 2023 Nat Commun 14:448 (CC BY 4.0)

**Design.** Big Papi (SpCas9 on U6 + SaCas9 on H1), all-by-all over 268 chromatin regulators, 4 domain-targeted
guides per gene and enzyme, both orientations. Lines: THP-1 (AML) and Reh (B-ALL), day 21 vs pDNA.
There are 35,684 unordered gene pairs per line.

**Data used.** MOESM5 (Supplementary Data 3) has three sheets:
- `300k`: the SLB measurement.
- `40k`: a 100-gene pilot library screened in the same two lines.
- `Validation`: a 40-gene library in THP-1, Reh, MV4-11, NOMO-1, OCI-AML2, OCI-AML3 and P31/FUJ.

Every sheet is already merged over biological duplicates and over the Sp/Sa orientations. GEO GSE215348 has the
RNA-seq data. For the screens it holds only a 40k count matrix keyed by H1 barcode, which does not resolve pairs.
The 300k counts exist only as FASTQ. Per-replicate and per-orientation GI therefore can't be rebuilt, so
`replicates()` uses the independent library re-screens instead: gi_rep1 = 300k, gi_rep2 = 40k,
gi_rep3 = validation (pair z-scores).

The five validation-only AML lines are not loaded as measurements. They have 780 pairs each, the genes were
chosen from 300k hits, and the panel is not genome-scale. Their mean z still recovers the SLB labels at 0.66
(30 pos; different lines).

**Label rule.** Positive: pair_z_score <= -4. This is the authors' synergy threshold; all such pairs have FDR < 0.006.
The authors' extra "lethal" filter (LFC below the line mean) is not applied because it adds single-gene fitness
to the label. Negative: |z| < 1. Everything else is null. EEF2, non-targeting and self pairs are dropped.
Six symbols were renamed by the resolver (e.g. WHSC1->NSD2, RFWD2->COP1); none failed to resolve.

**Results.**
- 71,368 rows (2 lines x 35,684 pairs), 268 genes.
- 341 positives (THP-1 158, Reh 183), a rate of 0.48%. 35,588 negatives.
- Paralog fraction 1.7%; 22 positives are paralogs.
- Own replication: labels from the 300k library, scored by the 40k library, give AUROC 0.73 pooled (THP-1 0.82,
  Reh 0.66; 29 pos). Scored by the validation library: 0.76 (THP-1 0.80, Reh 0.71; 36 pos). In reverse, 40k
  labels scored by 300k give 0.62 (70 pos) and validation labels give 0.87 (15 pos).
- Fitness-only AUROC: 0.58 (DepMap pan-line mean), 0.60 (the screen's own singles). THP-1 and Reh are not in the
  SLB line_effects table.
- Cross-study: no same-line data. In other lines, the paralog positives sit in the SL tail of the included paralog
  screens:

  | Source | AUROC | Positives |
  |---|---|---|
  | Dede | 0.84 | 4 |
  | Parrish | 0.94 | 4 |
  | Chou | 0.77 | 11 |
  | Harle | 0.69 | 4 |
  | Flister | 0.77 | 8 |

  Pooled over the included sources, the result is 0.58 (20 pos). The drop comes from non-paralog positives that
  are measured only in SPIDR (RPE1 CRISPRi DDR library), where they are neutral: 0.37 with 9 pos. Our score also
  does not recover the Flister labels (0.47 with 44 pos, max over 20 unrelated lines). Wong 2016 (OVCAR8, already
  excluded) gives 0.47.

**Caveat.** Between libraries, the z-score reproduces only in its extreme tail. Spearman is 0.05–0.15, and the
2%-tail split rule is 0.53–0.59 against the 40k library. Do not loosen the -4 cut-off: z <= -3 falls to 0.53–0.61.
z <= -5 replicates at 0.89–0.99 but leaves only 63 positives (0.09%).

**Verdict: include.** Own replication passes the gate (0.73 and 0.76) with a plausible hit rate. It is borderline
in Reh against 40k (0.66), and the other-line cross-study evidence is weak and mixed.
