# fong2025: Fong, Kuenzi et al. 2025 Nat Genet (SCHEMATIC)

doi 10.1038/s41588-024-01971-9. Supplementary files are under Springer Nature journal terms, not CC
(PMC12266356 is not open access). Raw files are in `data/raw/fong2025/` and the SHA-256s are in fong2025.json.

## Design
- Dual-guide Cas9 (hU6/mU6), 176 (position A) x 67 (position B) genes, of which 20 are in both positions.
  3 guides per gene, so 9 guide pairs per gene pair, one construct orientation. The library also has
  gene x AAVS singles, gene x non-targeting constructs, and 197 NT x NT constructs.
- 7 lines: MCF7, MCF10A, MDA-MB-231 (breast); CAL27, CAL33 (HNSC); A549, A427 (NSCLC). Each has
  2 replicates x 4 timepoints. The day lists differ between replicates, and there is no per-line T0, only the plasmid.
- 11,621 unordered non-control gene pairs per line, 225 genes. All symbols resolve through HGNC.
  Only 37 pairs (0.32%) are paralogs.

## Parser (`src/slbench/sources/staging/fong2025.py`)
- `load()`: score and FDR are the authors' per-line values from MOESM4 `cell-line-scores` / `cell-line-fdrs`.
  The multi-lineage, tissue and genotype pools are not used. The score is z-standardised per line
  (sd 1), and negative means synthetic sick/lethal. The 57 BioGRID reference SL pairs in Table S5 score
  weakly negative, AUROC 0.58-0.67. The 20 genes present in both positions give some pairs in both
  orders; finalize averages those.
- Rule evaluated: positive = z <= -3 and FDR < 0.05; negative = |z| < 1 and FDR > 0.25. FDR alone is not
  stringent. FDR < 0.05 occurs at |z| near 0, and in 4 of 7 lines positive-score calls outnumber
  negative ones. This rule gives 5-41 positives per line, 0.04-0.35% of pairs.
- **Shipped with `INCLUDED_LINES = ()`**, so every row has label null (measurements only).
  `load(labelled=tuple(LINES))` reproduces the evaluated labels.
- `replicates(mode="plasmid_last")` re-scores from the MOESM3 counts. For each biological replicate:
  LFC = last timepoint minus plasmid, centred on control x control constructs.
  f_g = median of gene x AAVS constructs. GI = LFC - f_a - f_b, median over the 9 guide pairs.
  The other modes (`first_last` = last minus first timepoint of that replicate, and `slope` = regression
  over the 4 timepoints) replicate no better. The pooled re-score agrees with the authors' score at
  Spearman 0.40-0.52 per line.

## Evidence
- Fitness replicates: construct LFC r = 0.32-0.76, single-gene r = 0.48-0.91. The interaction residual
  does not: GI Spearman between replicates is 0.01-0.21.
- Split-rule AUROC between biological replicates, both directions, is mostly 0.45-0.60.
  - The only line at >= 0.65 in both directions is A549, with z <= -3 in plasmid_last: 0.70/0.67.
    That rests on 11/19 positives, and the bootstrap CIs go down to 0.53. It drops to 0.58/0.56
    with the 2% tail, 0.62/0.57 with first_last, 0.66/0.62 with slope, and 0.53/0.58 with the guide split.
    I read this as forking-path noise, not replication.
  - CAL33 reaches 0.72 in one direction and 0.59 in the other.
- Guide-split pseudo-replicates (reps pooled, guide pairs split by index parity) give 0.39-0.68 and do not pass.
- Timepoints were not used as pseudo-replicates. Successive intervals share a timepoint, so their noise is anti-correlated.
- Single-replicate AUROCs for the evaluated labels (0.72-0.89) are circular, because each label is
  built from pooled data that include that replicate. They are not evidence.
- Cross-study:
  - A549 overlaps Shen 2017 (same lab, SLKB, itself excluded) on 1,055 pairs. Fong's score recovers
    Shen's 29 hits at 0.44. Only 1 Fong positive falls in the overlap.
  - Other same-line overlaps (Flister A549 / MDA-MB-231, and Chou, Harle and Ryan-lab for A549) have <= 7 pairs.
  - Any-line comparisons give 0.29-0.59.
  - Within Fong, hits from one line are recovered by another line's score at 0.50-0.61 (prior check).
- Fitness confounding: DepMap -(f_a + f_b) predicts the evaluated labels at 0.80 overall, and at
  0.85-0.89 in CAL27, CAL33 and MDA-MB-231. Flagged.

## Verdict
**measurements-only.** No line passes own replication robustly, there is no supporting same-line
cross-study evidence, and the labels are largely predictable from single-gene fitness. The hit rate
itself is plausible.

The check scripts were run ad hoc (not saved in the repo). They re-derive everything from `replicates()`,
`construct_lfc()`, `pair_gi()` and `load(labelled=...)`.
