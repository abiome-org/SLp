# kim2025: Kim et al. 2025 eLife 93921 (CombiGEM tyrosine-kinase all-by-all, MDA-MB-231)

Verdict: **exclude**. Details are in kim2025.json. Parser: src/slbench/sources/staging/kim2025.py (`load`, `replicates`, `checks`).

## Design and data
- 76 tyrosine kinases plus NegCon, 3 sgRNAs per gene, CombiGEM pairwise library, both orientations, 3 biological
  replicates, Cas9 knockout in MDA-MB-231. GI = deviation from a quadratic fit of expected vs observed growth Z,
  divided by the SD of the 200 nearest neighbours in expected Z (Horlbeck-style local z). RIGER p over sgRNA pairs.
- Supp file 2 is the only data table: 3,003 unordered rows (77*78/2), with orientations, replicates and sgRNA pairs
  already merged. Columns are observed Z, normalized GI, and RIGER -log10 p for Z and for GI. Hits are in bold.
  Supp 1 lists the gene grid, supp 3 has the sgRNA sequences, and supp 4 lists the GEO datasets used for
  expression analyses; none of them has counts.
- The raw reads are registered under BioProject PRJNA976939, but on 2026-09-25 SRA/ENA listed no public runs, and
  no processed counts are published. Per-replicate and per-orientation GI therefore can't be rebuilt, and
  `replicates()` returns an empty frame.
- After dropping self pairs and NegCon there are 2,850 pairs and 76 genes. All 76 symbols resolve through HGNC.
  39.8% of pairs are paralog pairs (TK family).

## Label rule
- Positive: the authors' 30 bold hits. The text says "GI < -2 and RIGER p < 0.01", but 40 pairs meet that. The bold
  30 are exactly the ones that also have observed double-KO Z < -5, so that undocumented third cut is encoded and
  asserted. The other 10 are null.
- Negative: |GI| < 1 and p(GI) > 0.05 (2,152 pairs). Everything else is null.
- Positive rate: 1.05%.

## Checks
- Own replication: not possible (see above). The authors report r = 0.50 between orientations and r = 0.74
  between replicates 2 and 3, but on growth Z rather than GI, and Z is dominated by single-gene effects.
- Cross-study, same line: flister2025 (Cas12a, MDAMB231) shares 227 pairs, but only 4 of our positives:
  BTK-ITK, SRC-YES1, FGFR2-FGFR4 and FGFR2-PDGFRA. None of them is negative in flister (diff_z +0.21, -0.09,
  +0.67, +1.29). AUROC is 0.42 (n_pos = 4) and score Spearman is -0.12. Flister has no positives among the shared
  pairs. With so few overlapping positives this is uninformative rather than a formal contradiction, but it
  doesn't support the labels.
- Cross-study, other lines (weaker): han2017 0.62, shen2017 0.19, ito2021 0.25 (n_pos <= 4 each). Also
  uninformative.
- ford2023 (MDA-MB-231, another agent) wasn't built yet, so it wasn't checked. It is the only candidate for a
  real same-line test.
- Fitness confounding: none. AUROC of -(f_a+f_b) for our labels is 0.51 with DepMap mean effects, 0.50 with
  DepMap MDA-MB-231, and 0.51 with the screen's own gene x NegCon Z.

## Why exclude
- 22 of the 30 positives involve FGFR2, FRK or TEK. The authors themselves say these genes are not expressed
  at appreciable levels in MDA-MB-231. TEK alone is in 10 hits.
- BTK-ITK, another hit, is a pair of haematopoietic kinases.
- Real genetic interactions between non-expressed genes are implausible. Hub-concentrated hits like these are
  the usual signature of sgRNA/noise artefacts in a small screen with locally normalised GI.
- There is no replication path and no usable cross-study support.
- Measurements-only isn't recommended either: the only same-line score comparison is slightly anti-correlated.
